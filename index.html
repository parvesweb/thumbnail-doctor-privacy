Privacy Policy — Thumbnail Doctor

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Thumbnail Doctor ("the extension", "we", "us") is a Chrome extension that analyzes YouTube thumbnails with AI and gives users a score, fixes and better title ideas to improve click-through rate. This policy explains what information the extension uses and how it is handled.

1. Information We Use

The extension only uses the information needed to deliver its single purpose:

  • Thumbnail images and video details: When you choose a video (or upload your own thumbnail) and click analyze, the extension reads the video title, video ID and thumbnail image of that selected item.
  • Settings and usage counter: Your preferences (such as language and panel size) and a daily usage counter are stored locally in your browser.
  • Free-trial cookie: The extension stores one small cookie (td_s) on youtube.com that holds only the free-trial start date and today's usage count. It is used to enforce free-trial and daily usage limits.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, YouTube account details or login credentials.
  • We do not collect or store your browsing history or web history.
  • We do not track your clicks, mouse movement or keystrokes.
  • We do not collect your location, health or financial information.

3. How Your Data Is Used

When you request an analysis, the selected thumbnail image and the related video title are sent to Google's Gemini AI API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) solely to generate the score, suggestions, title ideas, comparison or redesign you asked for. The results are shown to you inside the extension. We do not use this data for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Gemini API: Receives the selected thumbnail to produce AI results. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube image servers (i.ytimg.com): Used only to download the thumbnail image of the video you selected.
  • Payments (if applicable): If you subscribe to a paid plan after the free trial, payment is handled by a third-party payment provider. We do not receive or store your card details.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, except as described above to provide the extension's core functionality. We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, and we do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Settings, usage counters and the trial cookie are stored locally on your device. You can remove them at any time by clearing the extension's data, deleting the td_s cookie, or uninstalling the extension. We do not keep a copy of the thumbnails you analyze.

7. Permissions Explained

  • storage: Saves your settings and the daily usage counter locally.
  • scripting: Adds the Thumbnail Doctor button and panel to YouTube tabs that were already open when the extension was installed or updated.
  • cookies: Stores the single trial/usage cookie described above.
  • Host access (youtube.com, i.ytimg.com, generativelanguage.googleapis.com): To show the extension on YouTube, download the selected thumbnail, and send it to Gemini for analysis.

8. Children's Privacy

The extension is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the extension after changes means you accept the updated policy.
